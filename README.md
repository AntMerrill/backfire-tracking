# Backfire tracking

A working archive for **_Backfire: The Excommunication Story of Tim
Ballard_**, the multi-hour video Ballard released on his YouTube channel
(video ID `pBo_2DYxfrY`). The goal is simple: make it possible to check
what the video actually says, at the timestamp where it says it, instead
of relying on clips and summaries.

This repository is text only: transcripts, corrections and findings. No
video files are stored here.

## A note on accuracy

Transcripts are machine-generated (Whisper) and then corrected by hand
where errors were found. Small wording errors may remain. Before quoting,
check the timestamp against the video itself. Findings describe what the
video claims; a claim appearing here is not an endorsement of it.
