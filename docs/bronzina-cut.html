How she got here

Her grid as it stood on October 16: 3,202 posts since October 23, 2014. She posted alone for eight years; her first post with another account is April 2022. From 2023 a Latin American right-wing circle shows up (liberal.noticias, 169 shared posts; agustinlaje), and shared posts climb to about half of everything she posted in 2024–2026. She first tags Tim in February 2025, first posts with him in November 2025, and the rest of the coset joins after him: TB Rescue and the foundation in May 2026, expofamily.cl from August. Between October 16 and October 28 she blocked us.

Counts: bronzina_posts_per_year.csv, dates: bronzina_timeline.csv. "With other accounts" = she owns it with collaborators listed, or someone else owns it and lists her. What the 16 posts say doesn't split her six from the other ten; from August the expofamily.cl posts move from trafficking talk to Expo Familia and family-values framing.

John's casting: co-bots

John's reading, not a computation. In the one-monkey frame (at most one human per structure, the rest robots; John casts this one with none), timballard89 and sandrabronzina are co-bots: two robots in the same cast, seated by expofamily.cl, the arranger. The block is something the Bronzina account does as part of that cast.

The structure under the casting is computed: Tim is on all 23 posts of the two worlds and is their only cut vertex; Bronzina is on all 16 posts of her coset; expofamily.cl owns B11–B15 of the coset, and the posts of every recurring guest there fall inside its own. The casting itself is John's. The math does not show that any account is software, or who runs which.

What we can't tell

Instagram doesn't record who removes a post or a name, and it doesn't tell a blocked visitor what it hides. We can't see whether the 16 links still stand for everyone else. John says @sandrabronzina is still active and blocks us whenever she spots us.
