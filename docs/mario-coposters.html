How it was counted

A co-post is a post where both accounts are listed as owner or collaborator. @mentions in captions are not counted. Every co-post with its owner and role: coposts.csv (hash in SHA256SUMS). Two of the 18 (DdUQi6WRpU6, DdboK0DuIFy) came from a capture with no owner field, so their owner is not recorded.

What this does not show

Only what the public grids list: who is credited on which post. It covers mario's grid from Sep 10 to Oct 27, 2026, plus the 2025 posts that turn up in the timballard89 grid; earlier mario posts were not captured. A co-author credit says the accounts shared a post, nothing about who runs them or why. See also Same reel, two clips.
