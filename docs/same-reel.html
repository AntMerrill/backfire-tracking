How to check it

Download both reels, then run check_frames.py on them. It rebuilds frame_diff.csv (every frame's difference) and spans.csv. Scene cuts: scenes.csv. How it was made and its limits: README, hashes in SHA256SUMS.

What this does not show

Picture only: the audio was not compared sample by sample (both carry the same song by ear). It shows that the Facebook reel is this Instagram reel with additions; it does not show who made the additions, or anything about the people in the frames beyond what is on screen.
