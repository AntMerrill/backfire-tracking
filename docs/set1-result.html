Method

  • Scenes. Each video cut into scenes with dl_wm's cutfirst / scene_split.py; one still taken at each scene's midpoint. Stills are 720×1280 JPEG except video 4 (716×858).
  • Fingerprints. 64-bit dHash per still, compared straight and mirrored; the lower distance counts.
  • Threshold. ≤ 5 bits is "the same picture" — the operational reading agreed at 16:29, before the data.
  • Scope. Pixels only. No OCR, no transcripts, no audio; text-based work on the same posts is a separate object (Holland object: order 2).

What this does not show

  • It is not a statement about how any video was made. It is 40 midpoints, and whether they repeat.
  • A reuse that falls between midpoints, is cropped, resized, overlaid or re-graded enough can score above 5 and be missed.
  • One still per scene means repetition inside a scene is not separate evidence.
  • Different scene boundaries would give a different still inventory; the comparison tested this inventory, and the custody copies are what can be re-run.
