Short answer

DH22 is the set of 22 co-posts of timballard89 posted between 2025-05-01 and 2025-06-30 UTC, and the 28 accounts on them. It is not S16 / holland24 and not the rigid grid; those are different sets, and no number here is carried over from them.

On that defining set the construction finds one clean symmetry: accounts that appear on exactly the same posts are interchangeable, and the sizes of those classes multiply to 768 = 4!·2!⁵. One class of four and five pairs; the remaining 14 accounts are singletons. Over the captured network the picture does not become one rigid object: the largest look-alike class is still 4, and the non-trivial classes form a long tail. The headline is a small, exact, definition-level result, plus a regression check that the method still reproduces a known answer.

How it is made

  1. Points are accounts. A block is the set of accounts Instagram credits on one post: the owner plus the collaborators shown in the post header. Caption @-tags and picture credits do not count.
  2. Dedupe by shortcode, keeping the earliest taken_at (the house keep rule).
  3. Classes: two accounts are in the same class when they appear on exactly the same set of posts (identical membership rows). Accounts in one class are interchangeable.
  4. |Aut| is the product of (class size)! over the classes. This is the house time-order group: blocks stay ordered by time, so only account classes contribute. For the canonical object the blocks are the 22 defining posts; for the network object they are every captured post.

That rule is the whole method. It is the same construction used on 2026-10-20, unchanged. check.py (Python 3, standard library only) rebuilds the canonical 768 and the known answer from the included files.

Validation

The reused construction reproduces the known answer exactly: sandrabronzina, 282 accounts × 766 posts, |Aut| = 150,994,944, trio included. This is the regression check that lets the DH22 numbers be read at all — a method that misses a known answer is not trusted on a new set. Two unit checks (a synthetic four-block example and the known answer) were run before the real data; both pass.

The long tail

Over the union network (2,904 accounts × 98,432 posts) the largest class is 4, and there are 239 non-trivial classes: 186 pairs + 43 triples + 10 quads. Grouping is temporal, not random: about 88% of captured posts are single-account, and the repeated twins are a short, stable list. The 11-20 conclusion stands — the DH22 network is a long tail of small look-alike classes, not a few huge symmetric factors.

What it shows, and what it doesn't

Shows: who Instagram credits alongside timballard89 on the 22 co-posts of May–June 2025; that the 28×22 defining object has the exact symmetry 4!·2!⁵ = 768; that the construction reproduces a known answer; and the named repeated twins, with whether each survives the merge.

Doesn't show: a census of Instagram. This is a snapshot of captured grids to 2026-11-19. One defining post (DKYTBMSuTjl, 2025-06-02) is in no capture; two captured defining posts are incomplete (DKSZ5T6iU3E misses americatv; DLfpqobvgiT misses 4ludlins). For the archive view the owner is injected as the capture account (documented). Co-posting is a header credit, not intent, and says nothing about what any account thinks, knows or does off Instagram.
