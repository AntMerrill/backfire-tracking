What you're hearing

One note per collab post. The more accounts on a post, the higher the note: two accounts is a low C, each extra account a fourth higher. Posts on the same day ring together as a chord. Four and a half years run in 100 seconds, so 2022 to 2024 are a few lonely notes and 2026 is thirty a second.
