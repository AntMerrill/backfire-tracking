"King of New York" at the Eccles: open questions

Compiled 2026-11-13 from captures dated 2026-11-11. Every item below can be checked from the links given.

Alex Boyé's "King of New York" was listed by Live at the Eccles as a world premiere in Delta Hall (about 2,500 seats) on Friday, November 6, 2026. As of November 11 we had found no independent sign that the performance took place as billed: no review, no news report, and the show's own website did not load. The only "after" material we found came from accounts connected to the production. We are asking the venue and Salt Lake County to confirm what happened.

1. What the venue listed

ListingLive at the Eccles presents Alex Boyé, "King of New York": saltlakecountyarts.org/events/alex-boye/
Date and timeFriday, November 6, 2026, 7:30 pm (doors 6:30 pm). On sale from June 26, 2026.
RoomDelta Hall at the Eccles Theater. Salt Lake County's venue page gives Delta Performance Hall about 2,500 seats.
Credits listedDirected by Blake Sharette; music by the "Dreamspire Orchestra". A VIP package with priority seating and a Q&A with Boyé was offered.
Status shownNo "cancelled", "postponed" or "sold out" notice on the listing as of November 11.

2. What we could not find

  • The show's website, kingofnewyorkmusical.com, returned "name not found" on two checks on November 11, 2026 (the second at 11:05 am Pacific).
  • No reviews or news coverage of the November 6 performance turned up in a web search on November 11.
  • No attendance or ticket figures are public.

3. What appeared on social media

These are the only "before and after" items we found. All come from accounts tied to the production, so they show what was claimed, not what happened.

  • Before (about Nov 5), Instagram account latterdaysouljahz, post DfYkCDnsN9e (231 likes): "Alex Boye and the King of New York Musical… Tomorrow night Eccles Theater 7:30 pm!" The video shows a rehearsal room.
  • After (about Nov 9), Instagram account kingofnewyorkmusical, posts Dffn74OgTWx (22 likes) and DffmBPQAiaK (31 likes): photos of guests in front of a "King of New York" backdrop, caption "'World Premier' Gallery / Thanks for coming!", location tagged Eccles Theater.

A step-and-repeat backdrop and a location tag do not by themselves show that a performance took place in Delta Hall, or how many people attended. Screenshots of each post, dated November 11, 2026, are available.

4. What we are asking

  1. Did the performance go ahead on November 6 as scheduled?
  2. Was it presented by Live at the Eccles, or was Delta Hall rented by an outside producer? If rented, by whom?
  3. Was the full hall on sale, or was seating limited?
  4. How many tickets were issued, and how many were complimentary?

We are also asking Salt Lake County, which operates the Eccles, for the rental or presentation agreement and the box-office settlement for that night, under Utah's records law (GRAMA).
