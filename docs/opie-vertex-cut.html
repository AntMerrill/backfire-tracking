3. The cut vertex, symbolically

Definitions. S = (P,B,I), x incident with b when x is owner or listed collaborator. G(S) is the account graph: V=P, edge {x,y} when some block contains both. A cut vertex is v with G−v having more components than G.

PROVED (unique cut vertex of J). In J = W1 ⊔ W2 with TB identified: W1 and W2 share exactly the point TB and no block (the 10-31 J: core 7 posts, S16 16 posts, 28 accounts, 93 edges). TB is on all 7 W1 blocks and all 16 W2 blocks, so TB is adjacent to every other point on both sides. Removing TB separates the two non-empty sides. For any x ≠ TB, x lies on one side and is adjacent to TB there; after removing x, TB remains and is adjacent to every remaining point on both sides, so G−x is connected. Hence TB is the unique cut vertex. The computed check agrees: 28 accounts, 23 posts, 93 edges, G−TB has two components of sizes 14 and 13, cut vertices ['timballard89'], no bridges.

3.2 Edge cut / vertex cut, said exactly

  • The 8 removed posts are not vertices of the account graph, so they are not a vertex cut of it. In the Levi graph, B6 is a cut vertex (it isolates H,I,J); the 6 SB block-vertices are not a cut; the Laurent block is outside J. The 8-set is not minimal and is not one structural cut.
  • On the account graph, the 8-post removal alone is not an edge cut that separates SB from TB (SB still has 10 S16 posts with TB in the 10-16 credits). The actual 11-06 change set — 6 SB posts + 10 SB de-crediting + 1 Laurent post + 1 Laurent de-crediting — is an edge cut that isolates SB and Laurent. SB is not a leaf in J: she shares the 16 S16 blocks with the other guests, so the cut is the set of her 16 block incidences (6 removed posts + 10 de-credited), not a single SB–TB edge. On the full grid the same holds (SB degree 14 on 10-16).
  • The single account TB is a vertex cut of size 1 of J and of the full grid (computed). That is the cut that matters; the 8 posts are not a second one.

4. What cannot be told

Instagram does not record who removes a post, who removes a collaborator, or who archives. All the captures are from the same viewpoint, which reduces but does not eliminate visibility differences. "Left TB's grid" is a set difference between two snapshots; it is consistent with owner deletion, archiving, a platform takedown, or a change in what that viewpoint can see. B6 being off TB's 11-06, LDA's 10-28 and 11-06, and LDC's 10-28 captures (and claude reports it off LDA's 10-17) is consistent with any of those; it does not identify an actor. The statement that LDA's 8 borrowed posts are still on their owners' grids with LDA listed is claude's check, not re-derivable from the files held here. The SB and Laurent de-crediting events are visible as changed collaborator lists; whether the owner removed them, they left, or the platform did it cannot be told from these captures.
