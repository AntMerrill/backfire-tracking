2 · The letter

Three pieces: a short email to the venue, a formal public-records request to Salt Lake County, and a note to reporters. Each points to the evidence page.

A. Email to the venue

To: Salt Lake County Arts & Culture (Eccles Theater) · cc: ArtTix

Subject: Question about the Nov 6 performance in Delta Hall: King of New York Hello, I'm looking into a performance listed on your site: Alex Boyé, "King of New York", Friday, November 6, 2026, 7:30 pm, Delta Hall at the Eccles Theater (saltlakecountyarts.org/events/alex-boye/). Could you tell me: 1. whether the performance went ahead as scheduled; 2. whether it was presented by Live at the Eccles or was a rental, and if a rental, by which organization; 3. whether the full hall was on sale or seating was limited. If these are records I should request formally, please point me to the right office. The material I have so far is here: https://antmerrill.github.io/backfire-tracking/kony-evidence.html Thank you, John Daystrom

B. Public-records request (Utah GRAMA)

To: Salt Lake County records officer for Arts & Culture

Subject: GRAMA request: Eccles Theater, Delta Hall, November 6, 2026 Under the Utah Government Records Access and Management Act, I request copies of the following records about the event "Alex Boyé — King of New York" held or scheduled in Delta Hall at the Eccles Theater on November 6, 2026: 1. The rental or presentation agreement for the event, including the name of the contracting party. 2. The box-office settlement or final ticket report: tickets sold, complimentary tickets issued, and scans at the door. 3. Any house or event report for that night: attendance count, sections opened or closed. If any part is withheld, please state the basis. I prefer electronic copies. Please contact me before incurring fees above $25. John Daystrom

C. Note to reporters

Subject: Tip: a "world premiere" at the Eccles on Nov 6, with no trace afterward Hi, Alex Boyé's "King of New York" was billed as a world premiere in Delta Hall (about 2,500 seats) at the Eccles on Friday, Nov 6. Boyé's own posts sent people to kingofnewyorkmusical.com for tickets; that site no longer loads. I can find no review or news coverage of the night. The only "after" material is a few Instagram gallery posts with 20 to 30 likes each. I've asked the venue, and I'm asking Salt Lake County for the rental agreement and box-office settlement under GRAMA. What I have is here: https://antmerrill.github.io/backfire-tracking/kony-evidence.html If you've heard anything about the night, or would like what the county sends back, I'm happy to share. John Daystrom
