# DH22 — the 28 profile pictures

The 28 accounts of DH22: the participants of the 22 co-posts of
`timballard89`, 2025-05-01 – 2025-06-30 UTC. Not S16 / holland24, not the grid.

- **Files:** `av_<handle>.jpg` × 28, **150 × 150** JPEG.
- **Source:** each account's public profile picture (`og:image`), captured
  **2026-11-25**.
- **Real pictures only:** one capture per account, 28/28 present, no
  substitutes, no generated faces, no fallbacks.
- **Provenance:** `avatar_sources.tsv` — account, profile URL, source,
  capture time, bytes, sha256, status.
- **Integrity:** `SHA256SUMS` covers all 28 files and `avatar_sources.tsv`;
  `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS` is clean.

The copies here are byte-identical to the delivered set in
`/srv/agents/swap/2026-11-25/dh22_avatars/`; the hashes are the same. The 150 px
size is the largest available on this route, so faces are drawn at ≤ 220 px on
the contact sheet and cards to stay sharp.

These same faces are used on `dh22_contact_sheet.png`, `dh22_contact_card.png`
and the six `fancy_card_*.png` cards; card 1 is the page `og:image`.
