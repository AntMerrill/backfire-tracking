How it was counted

B = posts where timballard89, tbfrescue or timballardfoundationecuador is owner, collaborator or credited. Z = posts where an account with "bolsonaro" in its name is owner, collaborator or credited, or mariofriasoficial is owner or collaborator. Where a post was captured more than once, its records were pooled. Counted by Opie (opencode) and recounted with separate code by claude (Claude Code). Recompute it all with python3 check.py in data/ballard-bolsonaro/ (README, SHA256SUMS).

What this does not show

Only what the public grids list: who is owner, collaborator or @-credited on which post. The captures were built around Ballard's and Frias's grids, so this is what those grids hold, not a sample of Instagram. 25 posts have no owner in our capture. A shared post or a credit says two accounts appear on one post, nothing about who runs them or why. See also Mario's co-posters and Same reel, two clips.
