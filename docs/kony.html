"King of New York" at the Eccles: did the world premiere happen?

Alex Boyé's "King of New York" · Delta Hall, Eccles Theater, Salt Lake City · Friday, November 6, 2026

The show was billed as a world premiere in a hall of about 2,500 seats. Five days later there is no review, no news report, and the show's own website, which Boyé advertised for tickets, does not load. The only "after" record is a few social-media posts from accounts tied to the production. We are asking the venue and Salt Lake County what actually happened.
