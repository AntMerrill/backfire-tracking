How it is made

  1. Co-post = owner + collaborators shown in the post header. Caption tags do not count.
  2. The 22 = every co-post with timballard89 among its accounts, posted May 1 – June 30, 2025 (UTC).
  3. Contributors = every account on any of the 22, except timballard89.
  4. Order = first of the 22 posts the account appears on; ties alphabetical.

That rule is the whole method. build_list.py (Python 3, standard library only) is the rule as code: it reads posts.csv and writes the list. Run python3 build_list.py --check to rebuild it and compare.

Check it without us. Every one of the 22 posts is linked below: open it and read the header. Or start from scratch: open timballard89's Instagram grid, scroll back to June 2025, open each post from June 30 back to May 1, and write down the ones whose header shows more than one account. Step-by-step in the README.

What this shows, and what it doesn't

Shows: which accounts Instagram credits alongside timballard89 on posts made in May and June 2025, as of our capture.

Doesn't show: anything about what an account thinks, knows or did off Instagram; accounts that were only tagged or pictured; changes made after 2026-10-17 (deleted posts, removed collaborators). If you rebuild the list and get something different, tell us which post.
