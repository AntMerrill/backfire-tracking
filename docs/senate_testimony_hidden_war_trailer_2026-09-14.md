# The "Hidden War" trailer inside the Senate testimony — 13:02–15:46

Third piece screened during Ballard's May 2025 Brazilian Senate testimony,
following the same "Vídeo exibido a pedido de convidado" pattern as the
01:47:14–01:50:20 reel already documented. This one is the actual "Hidden
War" trailer/title sequence — longer, more produced, and directly invoking
the 2023 film "Sound of Freedom" by name. Frame-sampled at 2s and then
1s (and down to per-second fine-tuning around the two title sequences) across
the full requested range to avoid missing anything, per instruction.

## Why this doc exists

John flagged, before this range was pulled, that "Hidden War" is "featuring
Mel Gibson." **Correction:** an initial pass sampled the two obvious spots
for a cast card (the opening and closing title sequences) at 1-second
resolution and missed it, because it isn't at either of those — it's in the
middle of the b-roll stretch, at **00:13:48**, easy to step over at 2-second
sampling if the card only holds for a second or two. A finer pass right at
that timestamp found it directly: a clean card reading **"FEATURING / MEL
GIBSON,"** followed immediately by a dramatized seated scene with a
grey-bearded man who reads as Gibson, subtitled "Você pode imaginar como
seria ou como seria." John was right; the earlier "no cast card anywhere"
claim in this doc's first version was wrong and is corrected below.

## Structure of the trailer, in order

- **[~13:15]** A cable-news-style segment: a Black male anchor at a desk,
  "FR33DOM — FIGHTING TRAFFICKING" graphic top-left (mimics a news-chyron
  style, outlet not clearly identified — a "GBN" bug is visible on
  the inset screen).
- **[~13:19–13:22]** Grainy black-and-white dramatized shots of a man's face,
  captioned "O filme é baseado na vida de Tim Ballard" ("The film is based
  on the life of Tim Ballard").
- **[13:24]** Title card: "FROM SOUND OF FREEDOM" (small text) / "TIM
  BALLARD" (large) — subtitle "Você assistiu 'Sound of Freedom'" ("You
  watched 'Sound of Freedom'"), then "e aprendeu sobre os horrores do
  tráfico de crianças" ("and learned about the horrors of child
  trafficking"). Directly invokes the film "Sound of Freedom" (2023,
  Angel Studios) by name.
- **[~13:26–13:44]** B-roll: a man's eye in close-up, an ocean horizon, ruins
  of a bombed-out building, children playing/blowing bubbles in a street.
- **[13:48]** Title card: **"FEATURING / MEL GIBSON."** Immediately followed
  by a dramatized, seated two-person scene — a younger man and an older,
  grey-bearded man who reads as Gibson — subtitled "Você pode imaginar como
  seria ou como seria" ("You can imagine what it would be like, or what it
  would be like").
- **[~13:50–13:56]** More dramatized footage, a wall covered in a mural of
  eyes, a child being fed, a girl sleeping, a raid/handcuffing scene, more
  girl close-ups.
- **[13:58]** Title card: "AND AERIAL RECOVERY."
- **[14:04]** Stat card: "HUMAN TRAFFICKING IS A 32 BILLION DOLLAR GLOBAL
  INDUSTRY."
- **[~14:06–14:24]** A man in an "ATRCT" T-shirt speaking to camera
  (matches "ATRCT Media," credited in the other reel's production
  companies), a CNN-style chyron reading "UN warns of human trafficking
  risk for Ukrainians," and a pivot into Ukraine-war footage — explosions,
  smoke, rubble, a destroyed bridge.
- **[14:28]** A dramatized reenactment scene (not a news graphic, despite
  how it read at low resolution on a first pass) — a man in a lit room,
  subtitled "da Holanda" ("from Holland"). A fourth point of contact with
  this project's recurring "Dutch" thread.
- **[14:37]** A document/text card, mostly obscured by the subtitle bar but
  partly legible: discusses "withdraw[al]," "forbid all sexual contacts,"
  and includes the line **"I also enjoy touching young girls because I
  believe..."** continuing into a comparison with "homosexual[ity]." Reads
  as an excerpt from some existing text presented as evidence, not
  dialogue — source and authenticity not checked here.
- **[~14:39]** A stylized fake-social-media overlay: "0:41 · 119.1K views,"
  "11:43 AM · Feb 12, 2020" — presented in a video-player frame, source
  platform not identified on screen.
- **[14:42]** A website mockup: "The Pedophile Handbook."
- **[14:44]** A graphic: "ChildLove Pride Flag reveal," with a rainbow-style
  flag graphic (a previously-debunked hoax image has circulated under
  this name — not confirmed here whether this is that same image or a
  recreation).
- **[~14:46–15:12]** More dramatized/interview footage: an interview
  subject, an airport tarmac, a city at sunset, a hotel-room embrace, a
  girl discussing "maquiagem e peruca" (makeup and a wig), hotel-room
  writing on a wall, a silhouette at a window, a TV screen being watched, a
  couple on a couch, a man in a cap saying "chamamos Leslie e Martin" ("we
  called them Leslie and Martin").
- **[15:08]** A two-up "mugshot"-style ID card: names visible as
  **"Uittenbogaard," "Norbert de Jonge,"** and **"Lesley Lu[...]"**
  (partially cut off) over blurred/obscured photos. "Lesley" is close to
  "Leslie," the name on the mugshot card in the other reel (01:48:26) —
  worth checking whether these are meant to be the same person, spelled two
  ways, or two different people.
- **[~15:10–15:20]** More ATRCT-shirt interview footage, an aerial city
  shot, a night street scene with police lights.
- **[15:36]** A clip styled like surveillance/CCTV footage, with an
  overlaid watermark reading **"FISCO010"** repeated across the frame, a
  timer, and a crest reading **"FGJ ... [Ciudad de México]"**. This matches
  the visual style of Mexico City prosecutor's-office evidence release
  footage.
- **[~15:18]** Title card: "JOIN [...]," subtitle mentioning "Katherine" —
  not fully legible at the resolution captured; flagged, not transcribed
  further here.
- **[15:22]** Title card: "THE MISSION," subtitled "e quatro meses depois"
  ("and four months later").
- **[15:26]** Title card: "TO RESCUE."
- **[15:30]** Title card: "THE VULNERABLE," subtitled "tudo em quatro
  meses" ("all in four months").
- **[~15:32–15:40]** More raid and girl-close-up footage, then a clip
  identified on screen by a name plate as **Ernestina Godoy** — the actual
  title "FISCAL GENERAL DE JUSTICIA DE LA CIUDAD DE MÉXICO" (Attorney
  General of Mexico City) is printed on the graphic. Ernestina Godoy is an
  independently documented public office-holder; whether this footage
  genuinely shows her, in this context, making this statement, hasn't been
  checked against any outside source.
- **[15:42–15:46]** Girl close-up, then the title card wipes in over ~4
  seconds: **"TIM BALLARD'S HIDDEN WAR."** Holds clean through 15:46 and a
  couple seconds past it, fades, and cuts back to the live Senate hearing
  room at 15:49 — no cast or "featuring" card at any point in that
  transition.

## What this doesn't establish

- Whether the man in the dramatized scene immediately after the "Featuring
  Mel Gibson" card is actually Gibson, versus an actor, hasn't been checked
  against a reference photo — the card and the scene's placement read as a
  claim that it's him, not visual confirmation of his face.
- Whether Gibson has any actual, documented association with this project,
  "Hidden War," Aerial Recovery, or Ballard outside of what this trailer
  itself claims.
- Authenticity of the "I also enjoy touching young girls" document excerpt,
  the "ChildLove Pride Flag" graphic, the "0:41 · 119.1K views" social post,
  or the "FISCO010" CCTV-style clip — none of these have been checked
  against outside sources.
- Whether "Uittenbogaard," "Norbert de Jonge," or "Lesley Lu[...]" are the
  same individuals as "Markhijn" or "Leslie" from the other reel, or
  correspond to any documented prosecution.
- Whether the Ernestina Godoy clip is genuine footage of her in this
  context.
