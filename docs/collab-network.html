What the map shows

The host account latterdayavenger is the hub: linked to 121 accounts, with a total link weight of 1,450, more than any other account. Two accounts sit beside it — truthismytribe (830) and latterdaychad (792) — and then the graph falls away into a long tail of accounts seen only as collaborators, most of them one-off.

Total link weight is not a post count. It adds up the weights of an account’s links, so one post with five accounts on it counts once on each of that account’s four links. That is how the host reaches 1,450 from a capture of 354 posts.

How a link is counted

A post links two accounts if both are on it — the account that posted it plus everyone Instagram listed as a collaborator. A link’s weight is the number of distinct posts behind it, merged across every capture we hold. Instagram leaves the poster out of its own collaborator list, which is why a self-post still links the poster to the people credited with them.

What the captures cover

Of the fourteen group accounts, twelve were captured. partisanrocktactical and roofkoreanactual came back empty, so they appear only where other accounts credited them. Four captures stopped at our scrolling limit before reaching their oldest posts: truthismytribe, king__mosiah, priesthoodmen and mount_of_oliveslds. Their older co-posts are not in the graph.

Five accounts outside the group were also captured for other work, and their posts count wherever they touch the group: ldsabuseonx, dignidadyderecho, daily_dose_of_orthodox, letstalkhaydencarroll and sanctus_maximus. Together they add 8 links.
