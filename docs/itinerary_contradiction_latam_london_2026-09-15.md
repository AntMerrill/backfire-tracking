## The contradiction

Source A (2026-09-14, from Syria): states he is about to leave the Middle
East **for Latin America**, to execute a specific operation and to spend
"the next several weeks" there promoting Hidden War across Latin American
media.

Source B (posted within roughly a day of Source A): he is not in Latin
America at all — he is newly arrived in London/Europe ("we just got here"),
with a forward-looking European schedule ("meeting tomorrow," "next few
days... with critical leaders... NGOs"). No mention of Latin America, the
10-years-in-the-making operation, or any transit through Latin America
appears anywhere in Source B.

**This is not necessarily impossible on its face** — international travel
happens, and "the next several weeks" could theoretically still include a
later Latin America leg after the London trip. But as stated, Source A
claims Latin America starts *immediately* ("I have to leave the Middle
East... I've got to go to Latin America") and is where he'll be *for the
next several weeks* — not "after a stop in Europe." Source B's own
timeline (newly landed, days of European meetings ahead) is hard to
reconcile with a claim, made roughly 24-48 hours earlier, of already
being in and remaining in Latin America for weeks. Worth flagging as
impeachment material on the reliability of his stated timelines/itinerary
claims specifically — not a claim about the underlying trafficking
allegations either way.
